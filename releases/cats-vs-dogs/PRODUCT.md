# Neural Labs

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Zustand, Recharts and React Flow. The brief recommends React/TypeScript and related tools; Vite is an implementation assumption for a static, browser-local simulation with no server requirement.

## Users

People who want to learn machine learning by operating a simulated AI research lab, including beginners.

## Product Purpose

Complete a Cats vs Dogs image classification contract within a $10,000 budget by making data, architecture, training and deployment decisions.

## Positioning

The player owns every meaningful decision. Deterministic simulation makes the consequences understandable and reproducible without real GPU training.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Dataset marketplace and cleaning, splits and augmentation, editable CNN, cost estimation, training playback, metrics and experiment comparison, checkpoint selection, hidden distribution shift and multidimensional scoring. Every exposed interaction has centralized educational content with purpose, advantages, disadvantages and effects. Save browser-local progress. Future contracts and research progression are outside the first slice.

## Brand Commitments

Modern, technical, clean, slightly futuristic, serious but playful. Avoid corporate dashboards, courses and spreadsheet interfaces.

## Product Principles

- Educate without automatically correcting player choices.
- Model quality follows interacting configuration choices.
- Preserve completed checkpoints even when a player stops.
- Budget tension makes iteration consequential.
