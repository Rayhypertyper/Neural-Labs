# Neural Labs demo voiceover

1. Welcome to Neural Labs: a hands-on research simulator for learning how machine-learning systems are built.
2. Start with a simple question: can a model tell cats from dogs? The overview connects the research steps.
3. Choose a starting architecture, inspect individual layers, and adjust the network as your experiment takes shape.
4. Explanations surface model capacity and tradeoffs beside the controls, making every decision easier to understand.
5. Explore six contracts, from image classifiers to language models. Results are simulated for learning, and progress stays in your browser.

The video uses existing project screenshots and illustrative cat/dog photos. The narration is generated locally with the macOS Samantha text-to-speech voice. Captions are burned into the video and included as an SRT sidecar.
