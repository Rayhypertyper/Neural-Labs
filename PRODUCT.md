# Neural Labs

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Zustand, Recharts and React Flow. The brief recommends React/TypeScript and related tools; Vite is an implementation assumption for a static, browser-local simulation with no server requirement.

## Users

People who want to learn machine learning by operating a simulated AI research lab, including beginners.

## Product Purpose

Operate an AI research lab through six browser-local contracts, from a $10,000 Cats vs Dogs tutorial to a versioned language-model release. Learn how corpus, architecture, training, post-training, evaluation, and serving decisions interact.

## Positioning

The player owns every meaningful decision. Deterministic simulation makes the consequences understandable and reproducible without real GPU training.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the Cats vs Dogs tutorial and add handwritten digits plus four LLM contracts. Open research mode exposes all six contracts immediately, as chosen by the user; optional Career mode unlocks them sequentially. Each contract saves its own notebook and budget. The LLM workbench covers 28 stages: acquisition, cleaning, mixtures, tokenization, sequences, Transformers/MoE, distributed infrastructure and pretraining, continued pretraining, SFT, synthetic data, preferences/rewards/alignment, reasoning, safety/tools, evaluation/red teams/checkpoints, compression, inference, serving, hidden release checks, deployment, monitoring, and opt-in feedback into the next generation. Educational explanations accompany consequential controls. All training, prices, benchmarks, tools, and production behavior are explicitly simulated; no actual training or external data collection occurs.

## Brand Commitments

Modern, technical, clean, slightly futuristic, serious but playful. Avoid corporate dashboards, courses and spreadsheet interfaces.

## Product Principles

- Educate without automatically correcting player choices.
- Model quality follows interacting configuration choices.
- Preserve completed checkpoints even when a player stops.
- Budget tension makes iteration consequential.
